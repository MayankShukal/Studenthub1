Academic Norms

StudentHub is the unified digital campus portal for students, faculty, and administrators. It integrates attendance monitoring, event registration, class timetables, assignments, and campus notices into a single accessible interface.

Enrolled students, faculty members, and authorized university administrators can log in using their institutional credentials or student enrollment roll number.

Navigate to the Register page from the navigation bar or home page. Provide your full name, official institutional email, enrollment roll number, department, semester, and create a secure password of at least 6 characters.

You can reach out to the Student Support Helpdesk via the Contact page or visit SAC Block B Room 102 with your valid student ID card to request an authorized credential reset.

Passwords are securely hashed before storage using the robust bcrypt algorithm via PHP's password_hash() function, ensuring that plain-text passwords are never saved in the database.

Course attendance is updated at the conclusion of each daily lecture and practical laboratory session by the respective faculty instructor.

As per university academic regulations, students must maintain a minimum of 80% aggregate attendance in theory and laboratory sessions to remain eligible for end-semester examinations.

Visit the Events & Workshops page, select your desired event from the catalog, and click the 'Register' button. Upon registration, an electronic event pass is linked to your student enrollment profile.
